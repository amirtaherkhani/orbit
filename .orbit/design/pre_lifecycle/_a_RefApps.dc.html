Apps, app lifecycle and bookmarks

An app plugin is installed with grafana-cli and then goes through our Apps Manager: initialize, review, setup, validation, migration when needed, and enable. The Apps page (S12) shows every installed app with its lifecycle status and the buttons for that status. Bookmarks are separate: pages and sub pages of enabled apps can be bookmarked; an app itself and the tabs inside a page cannot. Steps 2 to 9 are our proposed workflow on top of what Grafana offers; Grafana does not ship it.

App lifecycle

1Install with Grafana CLIFrom the catalog or a direct ZIP URL. The CLI checks safe ZIP extraction and the plugin id and type, and installs declared plugin dependencies. It does not compare a checksum; the plugin signature is checked by Grafana when it loads the plugin.Server, CLI
2Restart and discoverGrafana loads the plugin and validates its signature under the configured policy. The app shows as Not initialized.Server, then S12
3Initialize and reviewChecks Grafana version and compatibility, plugin loading, signature, dependencies and the manifest. Checks that call the app backend (health, backend self-tests) run after Enable. An administrator accepts the review.S18
4SetupOrganisation, users and groups, sub page permissions, data sources and connections, app requirements, icon and colour.S16
5Validate setupRequired fields, permissions, connections, app prerequisites. Problems can be corrected.S16
6Migrate, if neededOnly when the app owns database tables and has pending migrations.S16
7EnableAvailable when checks, setup and migrations have succeeded. Right after Enable the first health check runs; its result shows in Manage › Health.S16, S12
8Apps and pagesThe app and its authorized, registered pages; page visibility and bookmarks. Hiding a page is not access control.S12, S11, S19, S20
9ManageStatus and health, activity, setup and permission changes, data sources, pages, update, disable, uninstall.S15
Update flow. Check the new version, install it with the CLI, restart, then initialize and review, review setup, validate, migrate if required, and enable. Rollback of the package and of the database needs its own design and verification.

Gates. Review → Setup is available only after the review is accepted. Enable is available only after required validation and migrations succeed. Manage is on the Enabled card; on a Disabled card it lets an authorized administrator recover or change setup. An error names the operation that failed. A hidden button never replaces authorization on the server. Still to finalize: exact labels, recovery actions, and whether every transitional state gets its own card status.
