Button zones

Button kinds

KindRule
Primary (white)One per zone at most. Always last in a right-aligned group, first in a left-aligned group.
Lifecycle colourOnly lifecycle steps (Initialize, Review, Setup, Migrate, Enable, Retry).
Secondary (outline)Everything else.
Destructive (red outline)Uninstall, Disable, Remove, Delete, Disconnect, as in Grafana (Disable is variant="destructive" in GetStartedWithApp.tsx). First in a right-aligned header group, last in a left-aligned footer.
Not allowed or not applicableHidden, not disabled. A hidden button never replaces backend authorization.
