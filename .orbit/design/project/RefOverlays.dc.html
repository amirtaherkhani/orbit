Menu sheet and popovers

Five overlays share one glass surface. The add-bookmark popover of the dock (S20) uses it too: rgba(30,33,39,.62), blur 20, saturate 140%, radius 10, a 1px top highlight and an opaque fallback without backdrop-filter. Each specimen is real markup from its board; the sizes are measured at 1440 wide. Position in the specimen is reset to static so they can sit in a column.

Their full contracts (props, states, events, keys, data) are on R12 · Component catalog and in .orbit/design/components.json.
