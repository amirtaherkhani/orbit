Menu sheet anatomy

The sheet opens from the dock Menu item. The rail lists the pages of the active app (for Monitoring, Grafana’s sections); the content shows the sub pages of the current page as tiles. Pages and sub pages are bookmarks; the app and the tabs inside a page are not. Specimens are real markup from the Menu boards.
