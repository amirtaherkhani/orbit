    1 pending

    Energy owns database tables and reports 1 pending migration. It starts by itself right after Enable, as Grafana runs its own migrations at start-up. With [orbit.apps] auto_migrate = false an admin starts it here. Its result is shown here.

    Scope
    • Only tables the app owns. Skipped when no migration is pending.
