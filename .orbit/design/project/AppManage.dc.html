How the check works
  • Runs when this page opens, after Save, after an app or Grafana update, and on Run check.
  • Passed: nothing to do. Warning: the app or page still works; the row says what to improve.
  • Failed: the app shows as Action required in Apps until it is fixed, and the row names the operation that failed. A page that fails is hidden from the dock and the menu.
  • Each failed row names the fix and links to it. Results keep the time of the last run.
